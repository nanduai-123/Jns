The Problem

Does this sound like your hospital?

  • You don't appear prominently on Google Maps.
  • Competitors rank above you for your own services.
  • Your Google Business Profile is incomplete or outdated.
  • You have few reviews, and they're unmanaged.
  • Patients can't easily find or contact your hospital online.
  • Your website exists but generates almost no enquiries.
  • There's no WhatsApp appointment CTA anywhere.
  • Social media is inactive or full of generic posts.
  • No one tracks which marketing effort brings patients.
  • Competitors are building a stronger digital presence than you.

If most of your patients come from referrals, that's valuable. The question is whether patients who search for your services online can find and trust you before choosing a provider.
